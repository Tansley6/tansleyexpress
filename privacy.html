Privacy

I don’t use cookies, tracking or adverts on my book pages, and I don’t collect any personal information when you look around.

When you buy a book or app, payment is handled safely by Payhip. They only share what I need to send it to you, like your email address. I will never sell or share your details.

Spell Ready

Spell Ready is my spelling app for children. It is set up and used with a parent, guardian or teacher.

When you create a family account, I keep your email address and the lists, nicknames and progress you add. They are saved safely with Google, so you can pick up where you left off on any device. Only you can see your family’s words. Please use a nickname for your child, not their full name.

To read the words out loud, Spell Ready sends the words and sentences to Google, and Google turns them into sound. Your name, your email and your child’s nickname are never sent with them.

Google may keep and handle this information on its servers outside South Africa.

If you record a word in your own voice, the recording stays on your device. It never leaves it.

No adverts, no tracking, and I will never sell or share your family’s information. If you would like your account deleted, just email me and I’ll do it.
