How it works

Set up takes about five minutes. After that, a few minutes of practice a day is plenty.

  1. Create your family account

    One account for your family. Sign in on your phone, tablet or laptop and carry on where you left off.

  2. Add this week's words

    Add your child with a nickname, then type or paste the spelling list from school. Homeschool families can add any list they like.

  3. Practise, a little at a time

    Your child taps Today's Practice for a short, gentle mix. Spell Ready reads each word and its sentence aloud.

Why Spell Ready is different

I built Spell Ready as a teacher, and as a mum to a neurodivergent child.

Many learning apps run on stars, points and rewards. For some children that works. For others, especially children with dyslexia or ADHD, it can leave them feeling they have failed every time a sibling or classmate collects more.

Spell Ready does not keep score. There are no stars to collect and no rewards to unlock. The colours are soft and the screens are calm, so children can focus on the words in front of them.

Each child works through their own list at their own pace. Grown-ups can see which words need more practice, but children are never ranked or compared.

Questions parents ask

If your question is not here, email me and I will help.

Is it really free?

Yes. Founding families use Spell Ready free while it is new. If anything changes, you will hear from me first, well before it happens.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser. Your lists and progress follow you when you sign in on another device.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Yes. Add each child with their own nickname and their own list.

Can my child change the lists by accident?

No. Adding and changing words happens in the Grown-ups area, which needs a grown-up to open it.

Does it work for other languages?

Yes. Lists in other languages, such as Afrikaans, are read by your device's own voice, and you can record words yourself too.

We homeschool. Is it for us?

Absolutely. You can add any list you choose, at whatever pace suits your family.
