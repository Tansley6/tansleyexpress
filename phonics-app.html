How it works

Set up takes a couple of minutes. After that, one ladder a day is plenty.

  1. Sign in to your family account

    Already using Spell Ready? Use the same email and password. New here? Create a free account in a minute.

  2. Add your child and choose a level

    Add your child with a nickname and an animal picture, then pick the level that feels right. You can change it any time.

  3. Climb a ladder

    Your child listens to each clue, builds the word with letter tiles, and climbs one rung at a time to the top.

Why Ladder Climb is different

I built Ladder Climb as a teacher, and as a mum to a neurodivergent child.

Many learning apps run on stars, points and rewards. For some children that works. For others, especially children with dyslexia or ADHD, it can leave them feeling they have failed every time a sibling or classmate collects more.

Ladder Climb does not keep score. There are no stars to collect and no rewards to unlock. The colours are soft and the screens are calm, so children can focus on the word in front of them.

Each child climbs their own ladders at their own pace. Hints are always welcome. Grown-ups can see where a little more help is needed, but children are never ranked or compared.

Made for every kind of learner

Ladder Climb was designed with dyslexia and ADHD in mind, and it works beautifully for every child.

  • ✓A clear, friendly voiceEvery clue and instruction is read aloud in a calm British voice. Tap the speaker to hear it again.
  • ✓One change at a timeEach rung changes just one letter, so children see how sounds build words.
  • ✓Read it backAt the top, your child reads the whole ladder, then tries a bonus question and a mystery sentence.
  • ✓Printable ladder sheetsPrint any ladder for the table or the car, with an answer key for grown-ups.
  • ✓Progress for grown-ups onlySee which ladders are done and which rungs needed hints most.
  • ✓Nothing to installOpen it in your web browser on any phone, tablet or laptop.

Questions parents ask

If your question is not here, email me and I will help.

Is it really free?

Yes. Founding families use Ladder Climb free while it is new. If anything changes, you will hear from me first, well before it happens.

Which level should my child start on?

Each level shows the school grades and ages it suits. If you are unsure, start one level lower. Early success builds confidence, and you can move up any time.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser. Progress follows you when you sign in on another device.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Yes. Add each child with their own nickname and animal picture. Each child keeps their own progress.

Do I need internet?

Yes, to sign in and to load the voice for each level. Progress saves online as you go.

We homeschool. Is it for us?

Absolutely. Use it at whatever pace suits your family, and print the ladder sheets for written practice.
